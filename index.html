THE SUB-LIMINAL

A first-person Backrooms exploration game, no objective but to document what's there. One connected space, no level select, no loading screens.

▶ Play

Enter the Backrooms

Dim lights, damp yellow carpet, mustard wallpaper that doesn't stop. A camera is all you have — three things out there are worth photographing, if you can find them and get close enough. Some walls hide crawlspaces low enough you'll need to crouch. Two things in here notice you back; neither can follow you through a crawlspace. Nothing to win, just however much of it you can actually document.

Real JavaScript and Three.js over a CDN script tag — no build step. Open world.html directly, or serve the folder with any static server.
