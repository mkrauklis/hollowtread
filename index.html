Enter the Hollow

You're a chimp with a flashlight, home in a treehouse above the treeline — the one safe place. Everything past the ramp is the same unbroken forest: hills, jump pads, three embers to find, and three things that don't stay in the bestiary room.

Real JavaScript and Three.js over a CDN import map — no build step. Open world.html directly, or serve the folder with any static server.
